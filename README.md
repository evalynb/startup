# Startup: Friendly Flowers
Startup spec for CS 260

### Elevator Pitch
Whether you are in a loving relationship or hopelessly single, Friendly Flowers is the application for you. With it, you can easily send your friends, loved ones, or potential loved ones a “hand picked” image of a boquet of flowers. Forgot an anniversary? No problem! Send your significant other a digital floral arrangement (with an optional heartfelt message) instantly! Missing your best friend? Roses fresh from the web are coming right their way. They say romance is dead, but Friendly Flowers is here to reignite that flame.
